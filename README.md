
# **JoJo's Bizarre Adventure (PART III) **
### Stardust Crusaders follows Jotaro Kujo, who, along with his grandfather Joseph and other allies, must travel from Japan to Cairo, Egypt, to defeat the resurrected vampire DIO. DIO has gained a "Stand" power, which also caused Jotaro's mother to develop a lethal Stand, and the group must kill DIO within 50 days to save her. Along the way, they battle DIO's many minions and Stand-wielding assassins.
